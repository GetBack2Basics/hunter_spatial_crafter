### Biography

George Chandeep Corea is a Geospatial Data Lead, cloud-native spatial engineer, and founder of *GetBack2Basics*. With deep expertise across distributed spatial computing, remote sensing, and enterprise GIS, George specializes in building automated, high-throughput spatial ETL architectures using Apache Sedona, Wherobots Cloud, PySpark, GeoParquet, and DuckDB-WASM.

His work spans national infrastructure siting, cadastral data modernization (GDA2020), state environmental monitoring (ECM/SLATS), and high-performance bushfire modeling on HPC clusters. As the author of *AuraSiting Crafter*, George engineered an open-source, multi-criteria spatial decision engine that benchmarks clean energy, water security, acoustic buffers, and net developable land across 15.91 million authoritative Australian geometries.

Passionate about open standards and transparent data-driven policy, George bridges the gap between complex spatial data engineering and executive decision-making for government agencies, energy planners, and regional transition authorities.
